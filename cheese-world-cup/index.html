Cheese World Cup

Enter the competitors

Each competitor brings one cheese. Add everyone, then make the draw. Any number from 5 upwards works; if it doesn't divide evenly, some people get a bye into round two.

    How it works

    • Two cheeses go head to head in every game.
    • Three judges, never the two cheese owners, vote for the nicest tasting. Two votes wins.
    • Judges are drawn at random from everyone, knocked out or not, with whoever has judged least going first.
    • In the final, every competitor votes. If it's a tie, the host decides.
